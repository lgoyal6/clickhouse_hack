Immigration deadlines · with provenance

Every number here tells you which rule set it, and the day that rule changed.

Anyone on a US work or student visa is running countdowns they cannot see. The rules move faster than the guidance about them: cap-gap shifted from September 30 to April 1 in January 2025, and most published advice still shows the old date.

Status Clock computes your countdowns against versioned rules with effective dates, so it can show you the exact moment the advice you were given stopped being true.

What it does

A calculator gives you a number. This tells you where the number came from.

Rules are stored as versions with effective dates and a chain of what each one superseded. That single decision is what makes the rest possible.

Seven countdowns

Unemployment days, cap-gap, H-1B grace, the AC21 365-day threshold, I-485 portability, the six-year maximum, and the OPT filing window. Each one declares whether it applies to you at all.

Nothing shows zero

A clock that has not started says so and says why. Not in H-1B status, so the six-year meter has not begun is an answer. Zero is a lie that looks like one.

Derived is labelled

Some numbers are stated in a regulation. Others are our arithmetic over one. The AC21 card says which it is and shows the working.

Unverified is visible

One rule in the table has a citation we could not stand behind, so it renders a warning band on the screen it governs. We left it there on purpose.

English, Español, हिन्दी

The people most exposed to these deadlines are disproportionately not reading English regulations. The engine returns codes, never sentences, so every explanation translates without losing its citation.

Architecture

Three systems, each doing the one thing the others cannot.

Not three logos on a slide. Remove any one and a specific capability disappears.

Postgres

Holds the person and refuses to let their history become incoherent. A layer-scoped exclusion constraint makes two simultaneous primary statuses physically impossible while still allowing cap-gap to stack on OPT, which is what the law actually does. Row-level security means a query cannot see another person's record even if the SQL forgets a WHERE clause.

13 versioned rules · unbroken supersedes chain · RLS enforced

ClickHouse

Holds 1.4M real DOL filings and every evaluation ever made. That history is what lets the product say not just where you stand but how you got here, and replay a proposed rule change across the whole population to find everyone whose answer would move.

127.8M evaluations · 20.6× compression · replay reads 0.08% of the table

LibreChat

The hardest problem is not display, it is getting accurate dates out of a frightened person who does not know the vocabulary. That is a conversation, not a form. Provenance is required by the tool schemas, so the agent cannot receive an uncited number and has nothing uncited to repeat.

2 MCP servers · 9 tools · citation required by schema, not by prompt

What we will not do

Invent a number to fill a gap.

The corpus covers H-1B labour condition applications, which skew heavily technical. Ask it about a home health aide and it finds three filings, so it refuses to compute a percentile and says why. A confident answer over three rows is not a percentile with a caveat. It is noise with a decimal point, and this is the one product that cannot afford to ship one.
