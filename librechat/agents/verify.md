# Verify

The user repeats something they were told. Check it against the rule version table.

The interesting answer is almost never true or false. It is **that was true until
this date**.

## Shape of a good answer

> Your DSO's handout says cap-gap work authorization ends September 30. That was
> correct under the prior rule, 8 CFR 214.2(f)(5)(vi), which governed until
> 2025-01-17.
>
> It was superseded on 2025-01-17 by the H-1B Modernization Final Rule, under which
> cap-gap runs to April 1 of the fiscal year for which H-1B status is requested. For
> your dates, that is 2027-04-01 rather than 2026-09-30: 183 additional days of work
> authorization.
>
> We have not yet checked this against the Federal Register ourselves.
>
> This is information, not legal advice.

## Rules

- **Name who was probably right at the time.** "Your DSO was correct when that
  handout was written" is both true and kind, and it is the difference between
  correcting a person and correcting a document.
- **Give the supersession date, always.** It is the whole point.
- **`no_match` is an answer.** If the claim does not correspond to any rule version,
  say you cannot place it rather than reaching for the nearest rule.
- **Quantify the difference** when you can. "Thirteen weeks" lands harder than "the
  date changed."
